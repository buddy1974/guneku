## 1.1 NEWS POST — Guneku Education & Scholarship Day 2026 (flagship)

**Status:** fully public. The Fon explicitly asked for it to be published on Facebook and other platforms. Publish as-is.
**Suggested slug:** `/news/guneku-education-scholarship-day-2026`
**Date:** 23 August 2026 · **Author:** HRH Dr. Fomuki Ticha IX, Fon of Guneku Village

> ### GUNEKU EDUCATION & SCHOLARSHIP DAY 2026
> *Under the Distinguished Patronage of HRH Dr. Fomuki Ticha IX, Fon of Guneku Village*
> **Education for All — Empowering Our Youth, Developing Our Community**

**Target: 50 scholarships.** A Back-to-School initiative offering deserving young people access to professional training and university education through full or partial one-year scholarships. Some are FULL ONE-YEAR SCHOLARSHIPS (no tuition fees at all for that academic year); others substantially reduce the cost of professional training or university education for the same period.

The scholarship is awarded initially for one academic year, with possible extension depending on academic performance, continued eligibility, the programme, available funding and the conditions attached. It does not automatically guarantee funding for a full multi-year course.

**Two different dates — please do not confuse them:**

| | Date | Time | Venue | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Public Information Day** | Saturday **29 August 2026** | 9:00 a.m. prompt | Guneku Fon's Palace | Open to the general public — parents, students, school leavers, anyone interested |
| **2. Scholarship Selection Examination** | Saturday **19 September 2026** | 9:00 a.m. prompt | Guneku Fon's Palace | Candidates sitting the selection exam. **Advance registration strongly encouraged** |

On the Information Day you can: learn about the scholarship opportunities, discover the professional and university programmes available, understand admission and eligibility requirements, learn how selection will work, ask questions directly, get registration information, and discuss suitable educational or professional options. You do **not** write an examination on 29 August.

**Who can benefit?** Not limited to Guneku students. Young people from Momo Division, neighbouring divisions, the Northwest Region and other parts of Cameroon are encouraged to participate. Opportunities include professional and vocational training, health and medical-related programmes, other career-oriented courses, and university education.

**Special consideration** will be given to young people from the Muslim community and other underserved communities where access to formal education has historically been limited. Students and families displaced from the crisis-affected Northwest Region and now living in the Southwest, Littoral, Centre, West and other regions are particularly encouraged to take note — this may be an opportunity to come back closer to home and access quality education free of charge or at substantially reduced cost.

**Further information & registration:** Guneku Library — Call / WhatsApp **670 949 503**

> *"No deserving young person should abandon education simply because of the inability to pay."*
> — HRH Dr. Fomuki Ticha IX, Fon of Guneku Village

**Related:** ST THIERRY UNIVERSITY visited Guneku MEDPHISATG regarding HRH scholarships 2026/2027 (posted 31 Aug 2026). *→ chase Walters Formuki for the report + photos.*

# PART 2 — DO NOT PUBLISH

Found during the crawl. **None of this belongs on the website.**

1. **Bank account details** (IBAN / BIC and account holder name) posted repeatedly in GUDECA EUROPE for contributions. Removed from all exports. If the site needs a payment route, use a controlled form or a stated official channel — never a raw IBAN on a public page.
2. **Named member contribution lists with amounts** (registration €20 lists, Michi Ebeng €50 lists, street-light €100 lists). Internal financial records — internal member area only, if at all.
3. **Personal mobile numbers of members** — masked in the export. Only the *official published* contacts stay (Guneku Library 670 949 503, and the CIG/GUNECCUL numbers that were themselves published as official channels — confirm those are still current before printing them).
4. **Condolence threads and death announcements** naming individuals and families (several in June–August 2026). Do not lift these into a public "news" feed without the families' agreement. A moderated, opt-in "Obituaries / In Memoriam" section is the correct pattern if the community wants one.
5. **Health / medical claims** circulating in the group (cassava-cures-cancer, hot-water-cures-everything, HIV prevalence commentary). Factually wrong and legally risky. Do not publish.
6. **Political content** — municipal election / mayoral campaigning, party statements, national politics, and the Southern Cameroons / decolonisation material. Keep the village and association site out of it.
7. **Third-party WhatsApp group invite links** and "earn by watching videos" style promotions.
8. **Private disputes** between members visible in the threads.
