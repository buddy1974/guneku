## 6. Handling rules before anything is committed

1. **Strip EXIF, GPS especially**, on every file entering the repo.
2. Many `RANDOM images-guneku-palace` frames carry a **"SHOT ON A56 itel DUAL CAMERA" watermark** — usable as documentary, wrong for a hero or a card.
3. **Deduplicate.** `chief-usa.jpg` and `us-flyer.jpeg` are the same image at the root and in `guneku-website-2023/`; `364041208_…n.jpg` appears in both USA folders; `Thumbs.db` and `New folder/` (empty) are noise.
4. **Consent:** these are community event photographs from the Fondom's own archive, taken at public events, which is a different footing from the WhatsApp material. Named-person portraits still need that person's agreement before a name is attached.
5. Convert to the site's existing three-variant pattern (`-thumb.webp` 480w, `-web.webp` 1600w, `.jpg` full) and the folder convention already in `public/images/`.
