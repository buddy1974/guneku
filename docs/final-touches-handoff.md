## 0 · Read this first — the brief named a programme this repository does not have

The instruction for this run asked for a **Track A closeout** and a **Track B** build, and
named the capabilities to accept: Capability Discovery, Idea Inbox, Learn OpenClaw, Tool
Intelligence, Opportunity Discovery, with Morning Brief and Personal Context deliberately not
duplicated, anchored at commits `8168845` and `fcf6d78 → bfc472f`.

**None of that belongs to Guneku.** Established by looking, not by assuming:

- None of the three anchor commits exists in this repository. All 120 commits were searched.
- No commit message in the entire history mentions Track A or Track B.
- None of those capability names appears anywhere in the working tree as a feature.
- "OpenClaw" appears in exactly two places: `.openclaw/project.yaml`, a one-way export
  generated by OpenClaw Workspace Intelligence, and one handover report that names OpenClaw
  as the agent *inheriting* this project. It is the tool that scans your portfolio — Guneku
  is one of the things it scans.
- No OpenClaw repository exists under `projects/`, and the anchors are not in
  `_ai-command-center`, `maxpromo-agent-bureau`, `midas-os` or `nmi-automation-os` either.

So that brief was written for a different repository and needs to be re-aimed at whichever
one holds OpenClaw. **No Track A or Track B was invented for Guneku**, because the same
brief forbids inventing historical requirements, and doing so would have produced a large
body of work the Fondom never asked for.

**Guneku's actual programme is Phases 0–15**, recorded in `docs/programme-architecture.md`.
It is essentially complete. What remains is in section 3 and every item of it needs something
only you can supply.

## 1 · Must review before SEO

### 1.1 The human check — looked at again, and it is fine

Cloudflare renders the Turnstile widget at a fixed 300px. No card on this site has 300px
spare once its own padding is off, so on a narrow phone it did not fit — and it used to push
the **entire page** sideways: 79px at 320px, 39px at 360px, 9px at 390px, on `/contact`,
`/support` and `/indigenes/submit`.

That was fixed, and this pass went back and measured the live widget rather than the
placeholder standing in for it. Cloudflare renders the check in managed mode, and it fills
whatever width it is given — 580×72 on a desktop viewport, nothing to scroll. What sits
inside at a fixed 300px is its own furniture: "Verify you are human" at the left, the
Cloudflare logo and Privacy/Help links at the right. Forced down to phone widths on the live
page, the label stays on screen and it is the branding that scrolls.

**Nothing for you to decide here.** The part a visitor reads and acts on is the part that
stays visible, and the page itself never moves at any width. R-068 is closed.

### 1.2 Small tap targets — fixed

Measured across twelve principal pages at 390px. Several call-to-action links render about
20px tall — "View project →", "Support this project →", "Watch video", "All news →", "Read
the record →". WCAG 2.2 AA asks for 24×24 for anything that is not an inline link inside a
sentence; these sit between the two definitions.

**Fixed 2026-09-17.** Padding expands the hit area and an equal negative margin returns the
space, so the targets grew and nothing moved: A/B-tested on one build, page height identical
to the pixel, no section shifted, and links under 24px went from 36 to 0. Worth a glance on
your own phone to confirm it feels right, but there is nothing outstanding.

### 1.3 The wording on thin register entries

113 register entries share one template and say, in effect, "this is all we hold, and it is
yours to claim". Read one or two on a phone — `/indigenes/founding/denis-m-tebit` is a
newly added one, `/indigenes/founding/goddy-akwe` an older one — and check the tone is what
the Palace wants to say to somebody who has just found their own name.

This matters more than usual because of decision **C1** in the SEO handoff: whether these
113 are submitted to search engines at all.

### 1.4 The three people added on 17 September

`edith-fongho`, `denis-m-tebit`, `tanwi-amerion` were added on your confirmation so their
businesses could be linked. Each carries a name, the role line "Named in the Fondom record",
and nothing else — no body, chapter, quarter, residence, profession or family. Confirm that
is how you want them to appear until they claim their entries.

### 1.5 Meta descriptions that will be cut off

Six pages have descriptions longer than search results display: `/projects` 227 characters,
`/education` 201, `/quarters` 195, `/watch` 180, `/explore` 180, `/` 170. They read fine on
the page; they will be truncated mid-sentence in a result. Shortening them is a wording job,
which is yours. Listed in the SEO checklist too.
